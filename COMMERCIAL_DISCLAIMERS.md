# Commercial Disclaimers

This document provides standard disclaimer language for commercial pilots and client handoffs. It is not legal advice. Review it with a qualified legal/professional advisor before using it in signed contracts, public terms, or regulated inspection work.

## General Output Disclaimer

Drone maps, orthomosaics, reports, AI-assisted findings, object detections, thermal layers, elevation products, and monitoring outputs are decision-support evidence. They require human review before commercial, operational, legal, engineering, agronomy, electrical, safety, or insurance decisions.

## Data Quality

Output quality depends on capture quality, overlap, flight altitude, camera calibration, weather, lighting, georeferencing, ground control, sensor bands, processing settings, and site conditions. Poor source data can produce incomplete, distorted, or misleading results.

## Measurement And Survey Limits

Unless the project explicitly includes survey-grade capture, RTK/GCP control, professional validation, and contractual accuracy terms, measurements should be treated as approximate review evidence rather than certified survey results.

## Architecture And Construction

CAD, BIM, design, or plan comparison depends on the quality and georeferencing of supplied reference overlays. The tool does not certify construction compliance, contract completion, structural condition, or regulatory conformity by itself.

## Agriculture

Plant-health formulas and field analysis depend on sensor bands, radiometric calibration, weather, time of day, crop stage, soil exposure, and ground-truth evidence. RGB-only vegetation outputs should be treated as scouting support, not full multispectral agronomy diagnostics.

## Solar Inspection

Solar panel and thermal findings depend on suitable capture conditions, equipment, flight planning, thermal calibration, irradiance, wind, panel load, and human confirmation. The tool does not provide electrical certification or autonomous fault classification by itself.

## Thermal Outputs

Thermal orthophotos and previews are review aids. Temperature values and apparent hotspots require qualified interpretation and may be affected by emissivity, reflections, environmental conditions, alignment, and sensor limitations.

## AI And Object Detection

AI-assisted issue detection and object detection are triage tools. They can miss relevant findings or produce false positives. A qualified human must review and confirm findings before they are presented as client conclusions.

## Monitoring And Change Detection

Monitoring comparisons work best with consistent repeat flights. Differences in altitude, camera angle, overlap, lighting, vegetation state, site activity, and georeferencing can create apparent changes that are not real site changes.

## Client Sharing

Client share links are bearer links. Anyone with the link can access the shared view until it expires or is disabled. Use expiring links, send them through trusted channels, and disable them after delivery or sign-off.

## Open Source And Licensing

This fork is based on WebODM/OpenDroneMap components. Confirm applicable open-source license obligations, including source-code availability requirements where relevant, before commercial distribution or hosted service launch.

## Recommended Handoff Wording

Use wording like:

```text
This deliverable is drone-derived review evidence prepared for decision support. It should be interpreted with the stated data-quality caveats and confirmed by qualified personnel before certification, construction, agronomy, electrical, safety, insurance, or legal decisions.
```
